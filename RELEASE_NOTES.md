# BarTab 1.60.1.18: the restock window

A /reload is enough after installing; no new files this time.

## New

- **Restock window:** `/bartab restock` (or "Restock list..." on the Crafting heading's right-click menu) opens a window of what your bars are short of, grouped by where to get it: your bank, your other characters, making it, gathering it, a vendor or the auction house. Ask... beside your other characters opens the ask window next to it, and auction house lines get a Search button while the auction house is open. It updates as your bags change. `/bartab restock chat` still prints the list in chat.

## Fixed

- Putting away into a full (or nearly full) bank reports only what actually went in, and says "Your bank is full; the rest stayed in your bags." Profession bags in the bank only count as room for what they take.
