# Changelog

## 1.60.1.18

- Putting away into a full (or nearly full) bank says what actually went in: BarTab checks each stack left your bags, instead of counting every one it tried, and stops with "Your bank is full; the rest stayed in your bags." Profession bags in the bank (a leatherworking bag, say) only count as room for what they take.

## 1.60.1.17

- `/bartab restock`: what this character's bars are short of, grouped by where to get it: your bank, your other characters (bags or bank, and whether you've asked), making it, gathering it, a vendor (with the cost) or the auction house (with the price, if your auction addon knows it).
- `/bartab ask`: a window of what your other characters have that you're short of; tick and set amounts to ask for it. They get a note at login and are offered it at their next mailbox ("asked"), or taken out of the bank for them, even if their own bars would keep it or mailing for others is off on them. A request comes off as it's mailed, and once you're no longer short of it (the mail arrived, or you got it another way); untick it in the window to cancel.
- Tidier right-click menus. A bar's menu leads with crafting (make materials first, Open recipe, Crafts); its rules (After crafting, Stop tracking, Count down, Buy materials at vendors) are under Rules, tracking its other ingredients or how to make a material under Track more, and Assign, Unassign and Share under More, with Move to Inactive and Untrack at the bottom. A profession heading's layouts are one "Show bars" submenu with the current one marked. The profession window's BarTab button menu leads with "Track all ingredients", with single ingredients under "Track one ingredient", the recipe's bars (Crafts, Rules, Untrack) under their own heading, and What to craft next, the profit calculator and recently removed bars under More.
- The sell window's notes are shorter, so rows fit: "you have N" only when you're keeping some.
- Selling is described by item quality (common, uncommon and better).

## 1.60.1.16

- Selling what you crafted: at a vendor, BarTab offers to sell things you made with your recipes (skill-up crafts like daggers, bags and bandages) that no bar uses and your other characters don't need, in the same window as the bank: untick what to keep (remembered) or change amounts. Only common and poor quality crafts unless you tick "Also offer green and better crafts for sale", so the rest can go to your enchanter. Food, potions, bandages and crafting tools (Arclight Spanner, enchanting rods) start unticked; crafting materials are never offered. "Keep a stack of each, sell the rest" (in the window, or Shopping settings) keeps a full stack of each stackable item. Ask, automatic or off, per character (Shopping settings); `/bartab sell` offers again.
- Crafts for your enchanter: the mail prompt also offers gear you made with your recipes, uncommon (green) or better, that no bar uses, to your character with Enchanting to disenchant ("to disenchant" in the prompt). Untick to keep, remembered like the rest. Choose the enchanter, or nobody, in Characters settings (automatic: the one BarTab has seen with Enchanting); "Send crafted greens to my enchanter" turns it off for a character. Soulbound items (and anything you're wearing) are never offered, and mailing skips a soulbound copy of an item for one that can go.
- The mail prompt remembers what you untick: the same item for the same character starts unticked next time.
- "Mail all I have of each" in the mail prompt applies to that time only. The Characters setting ("Start the mail prompt with \"Mail all I have of each\"") is what the checkbox starts as each time. The checkbox only shows when the prompt has materials it changes.
- Leaving a letter unsent (closing the mailbox, or Escape) no longer keeps the mail prompt away until a reload: closing the mailbox ends the letter in progress, and `/bartab mail` starts over.

## 1.60.1.15

- Mail on arrival: at a mailbox, the character your other characters mailed with BarTab is offered to take everything from those letters in one go (or, set in Characters settings, it's taken automatically). Only letters your own characters sent with BarTab are opened; other mail is left alone. It stops if your bags fill up. `/bartab mail take` takes them now.
- At the bank, after the take-out offer, BarTab offers to put away crafting materials none of your bars use (ore, herbs, cloth, leather, parts). It leaves anything your other characters are short of, and vendor-bought things like thread and vials. The offer is a window like the mail one: untick items or change amounts, and what you untick is remembered for next time. Ask, automatic or off, per character (Shopping settings); `/bartab bank put` offers again.
- Taking out of the bank uses the same window: untick items or change amounts before they come out.
- Bag space warnings: a chat message when your bags get down to 2 free slots and when they're full (Alerts settings). Clicking a crafting bar with no room for what it makes says so instead of trying.
- With "Send letters in one press", your BarTab craft key at a mailbox no longer starts mailing on its own: it mails only while BarTab's mail prompt is showing, and crafts otherwise. A letter that stalls is given up after a few seconds, so the key goes back to crafting.
- "Until I reach a skill level..." on a recipe that already has bars now resizes them for the crafts needed (26 crafts from First Aid 49 to 75), and brings them back from Inactive. Before, they stayed at their old size.
- Bars sized for a skill level have "Count down as you skill up" on their right-click menu (on by default): each skill point takes a craft's worth off, so the bar shows about how many crafts are left. Untick it to keep the bars at full size.
- Bars working toward a skill level keep their "After crafting" rule waiting until you reach the level, so running out of materials partway no longer untracks them.
- At a vendor, the offer to buy again while you craft there waits until you've run out of the item (the last Empty Vial used), not as soon as you have fewer than your bars plan for.
- The BarTab craft key starts making materials first when that's what a bar needs (smelting Copper Bar for a Copper Dagger), as clicking the bar does. Before, it said "Nothing is ready to craft."

- At a vendor, BarTab offers again when crafting there uses something up (the last Empty Vial): whatever's newly short, while the vendor's window is open. Anything you said No to isn't offered again that visit.
- Fixed: "The game didn't start crafting..." could show for a craft that did start, a moment late after another craft finished.

## 1.60.1.13

- Recipe bars now show one bar per recipe by default. Change the default for every profession with the Recipe bars button (Display settings), or one profession from its heading's right-click menu. Professions you've already set keep their choice.

- Recipe bars share what's in your bags between recipes in list order, like ingredient bars do, including ingredients without a bar: one Rough Grinding Stone counts for the first recipe that takes it, so the second shows it still needs one.
- Making materials first goes by the recipe's own share: Copper Battle Axe, below recipes that already count 4 of your 12 Copper Bar, smelts the 4 it's still short instead of saying it has enough.
- "Can't craft" messages say when recipes above it in your list already count the material it needs: "Recipes above it in your list use Copper Bar (you have 8; 0 left after them)."
- Fixed: opening another profession's window stopped a batch BarTab was making (smelting) without BarTab noticing, so the next click on the bar said it stopped instead of carrying on. The stop message now reads "still finishing a craft in progress".

## 1.60.1.11

- One bar per material (optional): a profession can show its tracked recipes as one bar per material instead of one per ingredient, "Peacebloom 2 / 3" for every recipe that uses it, which keeps a list of many small recipes (alchemy, cooking) short. Right-click a profession's heading in BarTab: Crafting for "Show one bar per material" (or set the default in settings, Display tab). Hover a material bar for each recipe it feeds and whether it's ready; click it to pick a recipe to craft, Shift-click to craft the first one that's ready, Alt-click to craft everything that's ready, one recipe after another (click again to stop). The recipes' own bars stay tracked behind the scenes, so shopping, rules, countdowns and the craft-next key (`/bartab craft`) work as before.
- One bar per recipe (optional), the third choice: "Minor Mana Potion: 1 / 3 materials", one bar for all of a recipe's ingredients, filling as you gather any of them (with 1 Peacebloom of 1 Peacebloom and 2 Earthroot, it's a third full). It counts every ingredient the recipe takes, including ones whose own bars are gone or were never tracked. Blue is the materials of crafts you can make now, orange the rest (so a full bar turns orange when you're out of vials, even untracked ones), and "Crafts: N" says how many you can make now. Hover it for each ingredient's count; click, Shift-click and Alt-click craft as they do on the recipe's own bars, and right-click gives the recipe's menu. Choose it on a profession's heading (right-click), or as the default with the Recipe bars button (Display settings), which now cycles one per ingredient, one per material and one per recipe.
- Vendor-bought ingredients (vials, thread, flux) no longer get bars of their own or count on material and recipe bars, since you buy them as you go; BarTab still buys them for you at vendors. Tick "Show vendor-bought materials on recipe bars" (Display settings) to bring them back. In the each-ingredient layout, a recipe whose only bar left is a vendor item keeps it, so the recipe doesn't vanish. Tracking a recipe with just its main ingredient no longer picks a vial or thread as the main one. Vials, thread, flux, salt, dyes and cooking spices count as vendor-bought from the start; anything else once you've seen it on a vendor (or your auction addon knows a vendor price for it).
- "Send letters in one press" (Shopping settings, Mailbox): clicking Mail in BarTab's prompt sends the first letter straight away, and at an open mailbox your BarTab craft key (or `/bartab craft`) sends the next one, or starts mailing without the prompt. The game allows one letter per click or key press, so each letter still takes one, but no more clicking Send.
- Letters to your other characters have a subject but no body text, so once you've taken the items they disappear on their own instead of having to be deleted.
- When a recipe can't be crafted, clicking its bar says what's missing ("Can't craft Minor Mana Potion: missing Empty Vial (0 / 1, sold by vendors).") instead of "Nothing to craft within this bar", and a recipe bar's tooltip says the same.
- Fixed: when a stop rule ended one recipe of an Alt-click "craft everything ready", the next recipe couldn't start ("Couldn't load Alchemy"), because the profession had been closed behind the scenes.
- Fixed: after a stop rule ended a craft partway through Alt-click "craft everything ready" (or making materials first), the next click said "Stopped crafting" instead of starting again. BarTab now notices the crafting has already ended.
- Material bars count every ingredient of your tracked recipes, including ones without a bar of their own, so a herb two recipes share always gets its bar (and Alt-click on it crafts both).
- A profession heading's "Untrack all" and "Move all to Inactive" include the bars hidden behind material and recipe bars and the hidden vendor-bought ones.
- Ctrl-dragging recipe and material bars reorders them: a recipe bar moves the bars behind it; a material bar keeps the place you drag it to.
- Ctrl-clicking a recipe bar's name opens its recipe in the profession window, as on any recipe bar (a material bar opens its first recipe).
- Fixed: an error at profession trainers ("Missing on/off parameter"), which kept BarTab from noting recipes you can't learn yet.
- Fixed: mailing your other characters skipped anything in your reagent bag (herbs, leather), so a letter could go without it, or a later letter come up empty. All your bags are used now, everything for one character goes in one letter, and BarTab says if it couldn't find something to attach.
- The mail prompt has a "Mail all I have of each, not just what's needed" checkbox: tick it to send everything you have spare of each material (after your own bars' goals), not just what the other character is short, e.g. a whole stack of 20 rather than 3. The list in the prompt updates as you tick it, and the choice is remembered.
- Examples in the guide, help, settings and changelog use placeholder names ([Alt]) instead of real characters'.

## 1.60.1.6

- The profit window shows what each recipe's item fetches at the auction house ("Auction for", after the house's 5% cut, from your auction addon's last scan) next to what a vendor pays ("Vendor for"). A new "Sell to" choice decides which the profit, per-hour and total figures use: whichever pays more (the default), a vendor, or the auction house. The price being used is shown bright and the other dimmed, so you can spot what's worth listing at a glance. Items your auction addon has no price for show "—" (and, with "Sell to: the auction house", are left out and counted below the buttons). Hover a row to see both prices and how many days ago the auction price was seen. The window is a little wider to fit the new column.
- Undo for removed bars. When bars leave your list (a stop rule or goal reached, "After crafting: untrack", Untrack, or by mistake), BarTab remembers them, and says "Type /bartab undo to bring back ...". `/bartab undo` brings back the last ones removed, `/bartab undo list` shows the last 10 and `/bartab undo 3` brings back the third. The profession window's BarTab button has "Recently removed (N)..." too. A stop rule that's already been met (skill 225 reached, recipe already gray) is cleared on the way back, so the bar doesn't vanish again straight away. Remembered per character, across sessions.
- Training reminders. When a profession reaches its current limit (75, 150 or 225), BarTab tells you which rank to learn next (and the level it needs), once per limit, and the skill on its heading turns orange until you train. Visiting a profession trainer notes the recipes it teaches that you can't learn yet; when your skill (or level) gets there, BarTab tells you, e.g. "2 new Leatherworking recipes to learn at Hahrana Ironhide (Feralas): Turtle Scale Gloves and Turtle Scale Helm." Turn it off in settings (Crafting page).
- What to craft next (`/bartab next`, which opens your profession if none is open, asking which if you have several, or `/bartab next first aid`; or "What to craft next..." on the profession window's BarTab button): the profit window, sorted by what a skill point costs. Each recipe's chance of a skill-up comes from its color (orange: every craft; yellow: about 3 in 4; green: about 1 in 4; gray recipes are left out), and its cost is what the materials cost, counted your way, less what you'd get for what you make, so a recipe that pays for itself shows a green "+". Two new sorts: "cheapest skill-up" and "fastest skill-up" (by craft time); the "An hour" column becomes "A point" with either. Recipe names in the profit window are now shown in their color, the row tooltip says what a point costs and takes, and a row's right-click menu adds "Make until I reach a skill level...".
- Shift-click a recipe in the profit window to search for what it makes at the auction house (while the auction house is open), to check the going price before you craft.
