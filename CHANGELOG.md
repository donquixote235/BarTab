# Changelog

## 1.60.1.11

- One bar per material (optional): a profession can show its tracked recipes as one bar per material instead of one per ingredient, "Peacebloom 2 / 3" for every recipe that uses it, which keeps a list of many small recipes (alchemy, cooking) short. Right-click a profession's heading in BarTab: Crafting for "Show one bar per material" (or set the default in settings, Display tab). Hover a material bar for each recipe it feeds and whether it's ready; click it to pick a recipe to craft, Shift-click to craft the first one that's ready, Alt-click to craft everything that's ready, one recipe after another (click again to stop). The recipes' own bars stay tracked behind the scenes, so shopping, rules, countdowns and the craft-next key (`/bartab craft`) work as before.
- Fixed: mailing your other characters skipped anything in your reagent bag (herbs, leather), so a letter could go without it, or a later letter come up empty. All your bags are used now, everything for one character goes in one letter, and BarTab says if it couldn't find something to attach.
- Examples in the guide, help, settings and changelog use placeholder names ([Alt]) instead of real characters'.

## 1.60.1.10

- Shopping for your other characters (optional; Shopping tab, "Show what my other characters need"): a "For your other characters" subsection under BarTab: Crafting with a bar for each material their bars are short of ("Copper Ore for [Alt]"), filling as you gather, for materials this character can get: ones it can gather with its own skills or make with its own recipes, or already has some of; your own bars claim what you carry first. When one fills you get the goal alert, and the mailbox prompt offers to send it. Needs come from their bars and last saved bags and bank, less anything you've mailed them. At login, one chat line says what you're already carrying for them ("You're carrying 20 Copper Ore for [Alt]. Mail it at a mailbox."). Right-click a bar to hide it. These bars aren't saved with your own and don't count for vendor buying, the bank or sharing.
- The profit window searches again by itself while item details are still arriving from the game (common the first time you look at an alt's profession), instead of asking you to click Search; recipes and material names fill in within a few seconds.
- Free gathered materials say whose skill it is: "from [Alt]'s Mining" for an alt's, "from your Skinning" for your own.

## 1.60.1.9

- The user's guide's What's new, Profit and leveling, and Managing bars pages cover the alts features.
- Send to your other characters: at a mailbox, BarTab offers to mail your other characters (same realm and faction) what their bars are short of and you have spare after your own bars' goals: "30 Copper Ore to [Alt], 6 Silken Thread to [Alt]". Mail fills in a letter per character (more if over 12 stacks), splitting stacks to send exact amounts, addressed to the character's full name (e.g. [Alt] [Surname]) with the subject filled in; you click Send (the game only sends mail on your click), the next letter follows, and BarTab says what went. Their needs come from their bars and their last saved bags and bank, and mail you've sent counts as theirs until they next log in. Settings (Shopping tab, Mailbox): offer, or off. `/bartab mail` asks again while the mailbox is open.
- Alts in "missing" messages: when you can't craft something, BarTab says which of your other characters can make a missing material and who has some: "missing Copper Bar (0/6, to get; [Alt] can make it; [Alt] has 40)". A bar's tooltip says when only an alt can make its material ("[Alt] can make Copper Bar."). Counts are from each character's last saved bags and bank.
- Alts in the profit window. "Include my other characters" (on by default) adds recipes your other characters know to the list, marked with who knows them ("Iron Thing - [Alt]"; the tooltip lists everyone who knows it), and counts their gathering skills too (a miner alt's ore as free). A new Profession dropdown switches the window to any profession your characters know, not just the open one, so you can check Blacksmithing from your leatherworker. BarTab knows a character's recipes once you've opened their professions with BarTab running. Recipes only an alt knows can't be crafted or tracked from here; clicking one says who to log in to.
- The profit and "what to craft next" lists update straight away when you change a setting (materials, missing, sort, sell to, the checkboxes), once you've searched; the recipe you picked stays in view. Search still searches again by hand, e.g. after a new auction house scan.

## 1.60.1.8

**Restart the game after installing (not just /reload): this version adds a new file.**

- A user's guide window (`/bartab guide`, "User's guide" in the settings window's title bar, or right-click the minimap button): every feature explained in sections, What's new, Getting started, Reading a bar, Crafting from bars, Chain crafting, Goals and rules, Profit and leveling, Shopping, Fill-up items, Managing bars, Settings and Commands, with a list on the left and a scrolling page. `/bartab guide chain` opens a section by name. At login BarTab mentions the guide once when it's new to you, and once after each update (opening on What's new); tick "Mention the guide at every login" (General tab) to see it every time.
- Updated help: `/bartab help` has new topics, goals (make until, rules, training reminders) and profit (the profit window and what to craft next), and covers chain crafting, the "could make" stretch, bank withdraw, undo and the Shopping settings tab. The README matches.
- Recipe difficulty on bar names: recipe bars show the recipe's difficulty next to their name, the way the profession window does, with skill-up arrows (3 for orange, 2 yellow, 1 green, none for gray), using the same icons as the profession window once BarTab has seen its recipe list. Settings (Display tab): skill-up arrows, the name in the recipe's color instead, or no marking. BarTab remembers each recipe's color from when the profession was last loaded, so it shows with the window closed too.
- Optional: the profession window's "Track Recipe" checkbox can track the recipe in BarTab, through the Track with settings window (ingredients, crafts and rules), instead of the game's own recipe tracking, and untrack it again; its label then reads "Track in BarTab" and it shows whether the recipe has bars. Turn it on in settings (Recipes tab).
- "Only buy for crafts your bars cover" counts what chain crafting could make, too: with 9 Thick Leather and enough Heavy Leather for 16 more on a 25-leather Thick Armor Kit bar, the vendor offers Silken Thread for all 5 kits, not just the 1 you have the leather for now.

## 1.60.1.7

- "Could make" on material bars: when you can make a bar's material yourself, a lighter stretch after what you have shows how much more the materials in your bags would make (8 / 10 Thick Leather, with Heavy Leather for 2 more: the last 2 light up), several steps deep, the same way chain crafting plans it. The tooltip says how many, and an alert (with the goal sound) tells you when you have the materials to make the rest of a bar. Turn the alert off in settings (General, Alerts).
- Chain crafting. Click a recipe bar when you're short of a material one of your recipes makes, and BarTab makes that first, then the recipe: "Making 2 Thick Leather first, then 1 Thick Leather Ammo Pouch." It goes several steps deep (Medium Leather to Heavy to Thick Leather), works out how many of the recipe your bags can stretch to (Alt-click: all of them), and does one step at a time, waiting for each batch to finish. Click a bar again to stop after the current craft. Settings (Crafting tab, Making materials first): make them automatically (default), ask first with the steps, or only from a bar's right-click menu ("Craft N, making materials first", always there when it applies). "Use my other professions too" lets a chain switch profession, e.g. smelting Copper Bars with Mining for Blacksmithing. When only vendor items are missing (thread, dye), BarTab still makes the rest now and says what to buy: "Making 4 Thick Leather now; buy 6 Silken Thread at a vendor to finish 1 Thick Leather Ammo Pouch"; the vendor prompt offers the thread when you visit one. When there isn't enough for even one craft, a click still makes what it can of the materials, toward the bar: "Making 1 Thick Leather now, toward 1 Thick Armor Kit (not enough for all of it yet)." When nothing at all can be made, BarTab says what's stopping it: "Can't craft Thick Leather Ammo Pouch yet, even making the Thick Leather first: missing Silken Thread (0/6, from a vendor), Heavy Leather (6/24, to get)." It names the materials one step down the chain (Heavy Leather for a Thick Armor Kit), not the raw materials at the very bottom.
- Tidier settings window. A new Shopping tab holds Vendors, Bank and Auction house (they were crowding the Crafting tab); "Mark tracked recipes" and "Training reminders" move to the Recipes tab under Professions; "Count down as you craft" gets its own heading, While crafting. Each heading now has a faint line running from it, so the groups are easier to tell apart, and the window is a little wider to fit the sixth tab.
- Bank withdraw: at your bank, when it holds materials your Crafting bars are short of in your bags, BarTab lists them and offers to take them out ("Take out"), moving only what you need (splitting stacks where it has to). Choose in settings, under Bank: ask first (default), take them out automatically, or off. `/bartab bank` asks again while the bank is open.
- Fixed: clicking a bar could say "Open your Leatherworking window to craft ..." instead of crafting with the window closed, for a bar saved at a moment the game reported its profession's ID as 0 (seen on a Thick Leather bar). Such bars now find their profession by name, and BarTab no longer saves an ID of 0.
- "Also track how to make..." on a bar's right-click menu now works when you already have enough of the material: it tracks one craft of the recipe (to stock up), rather than only saying you have enough. Use Crafts on the new bars to make more.

## 1.60.1.6

- The profit window shows what each recipe's item fetches at the auction house ("Auction for", after the house's 5% cut, from your auction addon's last scan) next to what a vendor pays ("Vendor for"). A new "Sell to" choice decides which the profit, per-hour and total figures use: whichever pays more (the default), a vendor, or the auction house. The price being used is shown bright and the other dimmed, so you can spot what's worth listing at a glance. Items your auction addon has no price for show "—" (and, with "Sell to: the auction house", are left out and counted below the buttons). Hover a row to see both prices and how many days ago the auction price was seen. The window is a little wider to fit the new column.
- Undo for removed bars. When bars leave your list (a stop rule or goal reached, "After crafting: untrack", Untrack, or by mistake), BarTab remembers them, and says "Type /bartab undo to bring back ...". `/bartab undo` brings back the last ones removed, `/bartab undo list` shows the last 10 and `/bartab undo 3` brings back the third. The profession window's BarTab button has "Recently removed (N)..." too. A stop rule that's already been met (skill 225 reached, recipe already gray) is cleared on the way back, so the bar doesn't vanish again straight away. Remembered per character, across sessions.
- Training reminders. When a profession reaches its current limit (75, 150 or 225), BarTab tells you which rank to learn next (and the level it needs), once per limit, and the skill on its heading turns orange until you train. Visiting a profession trainer notes the recipes it teaches that you can't learn yet; when your skill (or level) gets there, BarTab tells you, e.g. "2 new Leatherworking recipes to learn at Hahrana Ironhide (Feralas): Turtle Scale Gloves and Turtle Scale Helm." Turn it off in settings (Crafting page).
- What to craft next (`/bartab next`, which opens your profession if none is open, asking which if you have several, or `/bartab next first aid`; or "What to craft next..." on the profession window's BarTab button): the profit window, sorted by what a skill point costs. Each recipe's chance of a skill-up comes from its color (orange: every craft; yellow: about 3 in 4; green: about 1 in 4; gray recipes are left out), and its cost is what the materials cost, counted your way, less what you'd get for what you make, so a recipe that pays for itself shows a green "+". Two new sorts: "cheapest skill-up" and "fastest skill-up" (by craft time); the "An hour" column becomes "A point" with either. Recipe names in the profit window are now shown in their color, the row tooltip says what a point costs and takes, and a row's right-click menu adds "Make until I reach a skill level...".
- Shift-click a recipe in the profit window to search for what it makes at the auction house (while the auction house is open), to check the going price before you craft.

## 1.60.1.5

- A "Make until I have..." bar's tooltip says how many an Alt-click really crafts (only what's still needed), not everything your materials allow.
- "Make until I have..." bars no longer repeat the item's name inside the bar when the heading above already says it ("18 / 40 | Crafts: 22"), so the rest of the text fits.
- "Make until I have..." stops a batch (Create All, or a bar's Alt-click) as the craft that reaches your target starts, rather than after it finishes, when the next craft had already begun and had to finish too.
- One bar, one purpose: "Make until I have..." now replaces the recipe's ingredient bars with its goal bar (before, you'd get both, headed with the same recipe name), and "Make until I reach..." replaces a goal bar with ingredient bars. Crafts (on a bar's right-click menu and the profession window's BarTab button) now changes a bar's purpose too: "Until I have..." and "Until I reach a skill level..." on ingredient bars; on a goal bar, "Change target...", "Until I reach a skill level..." and "Track its ingredients instead".
- Fixed: a "Make until I have..." bar could show 0 of something you had, when another bar used the same item as a material and claimed it first (Thick Leather for a Nightscape Tunic bar). These bars now always count everything you own, and don't take a share from other bars.
- "A full stack" under Crafts, for recipes that make something that stacks: sizes the bars for the crafts that make one full stack (10 Heavy Armor Kits: 10 crafts).
- "Other amount..." under Crafts (on a bar's right-click menu, and the profession window's BarTab button) sizes the recipe's bars for any number of crafts, e.g. 25 Heavy Armor Kits.
- Tidier menus. The profession window's BarTab button groups its entries under Track (each ingredient, All ingredients, With settings...), Goals (Make until I have..., Make until I reach...) and, once the recipe has bars, Its bars (Crafts, After crafting, Count down, Stop tracking, Untrack this recipe), with the profit calculator at the bottom. A bar's right-click menu groups its entries under Crafting, Rules and This bar.
- The profit window (`/bartab profit`, or "Profit calculator..." on the profession window's BarTab button): choose how to count materials you have (free, at auction price, or at vendor price) and materials you're missing (buy them, buy or make them when cheaper, or leave those recipes out), whether to count items from your gathering skills as free (leather and hides you can skin, ore and stone you can mine, herbs you can pick; not things that only drop from monsters, like murloc scales; on by default), whether to show only recipes you can craft now and whether to include gray ones, and how to sort (profit per craft, per hour, or in total for what you can make now). Search fills a list of the open profession's recipes with what each sells for, costs and earns, and how many you can make. Hover a row for its cost breakdown, click it to show the recipe, right-click to track it or to choose, per material, how to count it: Automatic (from your settings), Use my own, or Buy it (compare Turtle Scale Breastplate with your own Turtle Scale and with bought ones; Elemental Earth isn't free just because some is in your bags). The list updates straight away, and the choice applies to that material in every recipe. The recipe you last clicked is shaded green and kept in view when a change moves it. "Material choices (N)" next to Search lists every material you've set, to switch any of them or reset them all to automatic. Your choices are remembered. `/bartab profit list` still prints the short version in chat.

## 1.60.1.4

- "Make until I have..." bars show how far the materials in your bags would take them: a lighter stretch after what you have (2 Coarse Stone on a 0 / 51 Coarse Blasting Powder bar lights up the next 2). Their tooltip lists the ingredients (have / need) even for one-ingredient recipes, and says how many more you can make.
- "Stop tracking at skill N" with an orange recipe: the batch now stops as the craft that reaches N starts, so it doesn't begin one more craft. (Before, the skill-up arrived after the next craft had already started; addons can't cancel a craft in progress, so that one finished.) Yellow and green recipes don't always give a point, so they still stop on the skill-up, which can mean one extra craft.
- Appearances: closing the "which ingredients?" window (with X, or Escape, which closes every open window) no longer stops BarTab offering recipes until your next login. A recipe you've just learned is always offered, and recipes from a scan are offered again the next time you open a profession window yourself.
- Less background work: BarTab no longer reacts to the game re-applying a tracker section's collapsed state during a redraw (which could set off one redraw after another), forces at most one tracker redraw a second, and only watches the Shift and Alt keys while the mouse is over a bar.
- `/bartab profit` (with a profession open): which of its recipes earn the most when you sell what they make to a vendor. Two lists: using materials you have (only vendor-bought ingredients like thread cost anything), and buying the other materials at auction prices (from an auction addon, if you have one and have scanned). A material one of your recipes makes (Copper Bar from ore, Thick Leather from Heavy Leather) is priced at whichever is cheaper, buying it or making it from bought ingredients, following chains several steps deep (ore to bar to part), and the line says which materials you'd make. Each shows the profit per craft, and per hour where BarTab knows how long the craft takes. Vendor prices are noted as you visit vendors.
- How long crafts take: bar tooltips show it for each click ("Alt-click: craft 4 (maximum available, about 4 min)"), a batch of 30 seconds or more says so in chat when it starts, and "Make until I reach..." bars add it to their crafts-left line. BarTab uses the time it last saw the recipe take, or the game's cast time until then.
- Fixed: a stop rule reached in the middle of a batch crafted with the profession window closed removed the bars but let the batch carry on. Asking the game to stop repeating the craft isn't always enough, so BarTab now also closes the profession it opened in the background, which ends the batch (the craft being cast still finishes).
- Fixed: a batch crafted from a bar with the profession window closed could stop after one craft when a craft takes a long time (20 seconds or more): BarTab thought the batch had ended and closed the profession it had opened in the background, which ends the batch. It now waits while you're still casting or the game is still repeating the craft.
- Fixed: a recipe first tracked for its appearance and then set up with "Make until I reach..." or "Track with settings..." kept the appearance rule, so its bars were removed as soon as the craft collected the look. Those bars now become ordinary bars.
- Fixed: new counts (leather you just looted) could take a while to show on the bars. BarTab asks the tracker to redraw, but in this client the tracker doesn't always act on that, so the bars waited for something else to redraw it. BarTab now checks a moment later and redraws its sections itself if they're still waiting (after combat if you're fighting).
- Fixed: the objective tracker could stop updating (bars not changing, a missing bar, or BarTab's section stuck with a gap above it) until a /reload, most often after a level-up. The game's tracker can get stuck thinking a redraw is already on its way, and then never redraws. BarTab now notices when that lasts more than a few seconds and runs the redraw itself (after combat if you're fighting). A level-up's precautionary redraw now does the same.

## 1.60.1.2

BarTab's version now follows WoW Forever's (1.60.1), plus a number for BarTab's own releases. 1.6 was the first public release (1.60.1.1).

- Make until you reach a skill level: "Make until I reach..." on the profession window's BarTab button asks for a level (the next multiple of 5 by default), then which ingredients to track (the main one ticked; tick the rest for all of them), sized at one craft per skill point still needed. The bars get "Stop tracking at skill" at that level; click one to craft, and the batch stops and the bars clear themselves when you get there. The bars resize with your skill rather than counting down: a craft that gives a skill point shrinks them by one craft, and one that doesn't leaves them as they are. Picking a size from the Crafts submenu switches them back to normal bars.
- Make until you have N: "Make until I have..." on the profession window's BarTab button (or `/bartab make 52` with the recipe selected) adds a bar for what the recipe makes, e.g. "Handful of Copper Bolts 12/52". Shift-click and Alt-click on it craft only what's still missing, and a Create All batch from the profession window stops once you have that many. Right-click the bar to change the number.
- Fixed: with "Stop tracking at skill N", reaching the skill in the middle of a batch (an Alt-click, or Create All in the profession window) removed the bars but the game kept crafting. BarTab now stops the batch when the rule is reached, and says so. Skill-ups are also checked right away, so the batch stops before its next craft.
- `/bartab refresh` redraws BarTab's tracker sections if they stop updating, without a /reload. It also saves how the tracker looked before and after, for bug reports. BarTab saves the same details shortly after each level-up and then redraws its sections as a precaution, since the tracker has stopped updating after a level-up a few times.
- A level-up no longer runs BarTab's skill checks once for every weapon and defense skill that changes at the same moment; it checks once, a second later. Your combat abilities are no longer treated as possible crafts or written to the craft log.
- The profession window's BarTab button now has the recipe settings from a bar's right-click menu once the selected recipe has bars: Crafts, After crafting, Count down as you craft, Stop tracking, and "Untrack this recipe" (all of its bars at once). After crafting set from there applies to every bar of the recipe. The button shows a check mark while the selected recipe has bars.
- Bars from "Make until I reach..." say how many crafts are left in their tooltip: "At least 14 crafts to Leatherworking 75".

## 1.6 (1.60.1.1, first public release)

- "Crafts" on a recipe bar's right-click menu sizes all of the recipe's bars at once: 1, 5 or 10 crafts, or the crafts it takes to reach the next multiple of 5 in the profession ("To Leatherworking 165 (3 crafts)" at 162), or "Use all your Heavy Leather" (37 at 8 a craft: 4 crafts). Resizing never sets off a goal alert.
- Recipe items: a pattern, recipe, plans, formula or schematic for one of your professions that you haven't learned says "BarTab: Alt-Shift-click to add this recipe to your Inactive list" in its tooltip (at a vendor or in your bags; not in the auction house window, where that click opens the item's listings). Alt-Shift-clicking it tracks the recipe like the trainer does: in Inactive, marked not learned, active once you learn it. Works once you've opened that profession with this version.
- "Include Inactive bars in the list" (Crafting tab, under Auction house; off by default): the auction house shopping list also covers your Inactive bars, such as shelved recipes or ones you haven't learned yet from the trainer.
- Track recipes from the trainer: Alt-Shift-click a recipe in a profession trainer's list (or select it and type `/bartab trainer`) to track its ingredients, even before you've learned it. A window asks which ingredients. Recipes you haven't learned wait in Inactive, marked "(not learned)" with the skill they need; they can't be crafted and vendor buying skips them. Learning the recipe makes them ordinary active bars. Hovering a trainer recipe says so: "BarTab: Alt-Shift-click to add this recipe to your Inactive list" (or "to track this recipe" if you know it, or "tracking this recipe").
- A Waylaid Crate in your bags says "BarTab: Alt-Shift-click to track this crate" in its tooltip (or "BarTab: tracking this crate" once it is). Follows the "Show BarTab lines on item tooltips" setting. Crates are only tracked from your bags: one that's only in your bank says "BarTab: move this crate to your bags, then Alt-Shift-click it if it doesn't track automatically", and Alt-Shift-clicking it there says the same in chat.
- "Count down as you craft" on a recipe bar's right-click menu (and a default on the Crafting tab, off unless you turn it on): each craft takes one craft's worth off the recipe's bars, so they show what's left to make (8/32 becomes 4/28, not 4/32). The After crafting rule then waits until the count runs out; bars kept by the rule move to Inactive at full size, ready for next time. Waits for the bag update like the After crafting rule, so it never sets off a false goal alert. Shared bar strings carry it.
- Ctrl-click a recipe bar (or its name) to open the profession window on that recipe; also "Open recipe" on the bar's right-click menu. Ctrl-drag still reorders.
- Favorite recipes: `/bartab favorites` or "Import favorites" (new Recipes tab in settings) tracks the open profession's starred recipes that aren't tracked yet, one craft each, through the same window as appearances (main ingredients, ALL INGREDIENTS, or choose each). "Track recipes when you favorite them" (off by default) offers a recipe as soon as you star it.
- Appearances: "Track recipes for appearances you haven't collected" (Recipes tab, or `/bartab appearances on`). When you learn a recipe or open a profession, recipes that make gear with an appearance this character can collect but hasn't get bars for one craft, set to untrack after crafting. A window asks which ingredients to track (the main one, needed most, is ticked); "Not this one" skips the recipe, and closing the window asks again later. Settings can switch this to the main ingredient only, or every ingredient. Each recipe is offered once (untrack it and it stays gone), recipes you already have a crafted copy of (bags, bank or an alt) are skipped, and only orange and yellow recipes count unless "Include recipes that no longer give skill" is on. When several recipes qualify at once (usually the first scan), a single summary window lists them instead: Track main ingredients (or tick ALL INGREDIENTS), Choose each, Skip all, or Later (asks again next login, or with `/bartab appearances scan`). "Add appearance bars to Inactive" (off by default) puts new appearance bars in the profession's Inactive area. After crafting one, BarTab reminds you to equip or disenchant it, since crafting alone doesn't collect the appearance. If you collect an appearance some other way (loot, purchase, an alt), its bars are untracked too.
- Commands are now `/bartab`, with `/btab` as a short form. `/bt` still works unless another addon uses it, in which case BarTab leaves it to that addon and says so once.
- Fixed: when "After crafting" untracked (or shelved) a recipe's bars, another bar sharing the same material could briefly count the materials just used up, and fire a false goal alert. The rule now waits for your bags to update after the craft.
- Fixed: a bar made before the game had loaded its item could be stuck with a name like "item 1529". BarTab now renames it once the name is known (at login, or as soon as the item loads), merging it into an existing bar for the same thing if there is one. Windows that list ingredients also wait a moment for their names.
- Fixed: untracking a crate's bars didn't stick; the next bag change (even moving a crate to the bank) tracked it again. BarTab now remembers untracked crates by how many are in your bags: crates leaving your bags (used, sold or banked) don't bring tracking back, a crate coming in (looted, or taken back out of the bank) does, and `/bartab item` adds one back by hand.
- Fixed: Waylaid Crates weren't detected on clients where the game's tooltip data comes back empty. BarTab now falls back to reading the item's tooltip directly, and accepts the fill list whether it's inside the "Use:" line or on the lines after it. `/bartab debug item <name>` now also says where it read the tooltip from and what BarTab made of it.
- Fixed: a Waylaid Crate the game hadn't loaded yet (for example one bought at the auction house and taken from the mailbox) could be missed until /reload. BarTab now waits for it to load and adds it then.
- Quest tracking removed: the BarTab: Quests section, quest auto-tracking, bars under quests in the quest tracker, and clicking quest items from bars are gone, along with `/bt quests`, `/bt auto` and `/bt inline`. They added little over the game's own quest tracker and were the riskiest part of BarTab. Existing quest bars and their settings are cleared at login. Crafting, fill-up crates and the Items section are unchanged; the "Quests & Items" settings tab is now "Items", and `/bt help items` replaces `/bt help quests`.
- Fixed: after a login where the game reported your realm a moment late, BarTab could start an empty bar list for "Name-" (no realm) instead of using yours, so all your bars seemed to vanish. BarTab now waits until the game has reported your name and realm before picking your list, and merges any list saved under a missing realm back into yours at login (it says how many bars it recovered).
- BarTab now also puts back any of its sections the objective tracker drops, keeps trying to join the tracker for about 30 seconds after login, and `/bartab debug tracker` lists how each bar is being shown, for bug reports.
- Safer tracker: an error in BarTab's part of the objective tracker update can no longer stop the whole tracker from updating (which could freeze your quest list until /reload). Errors are kept instead; `/bt errors` shows the last few.
- Fixed: bars could show twice, in the tracker and in a plain list below it, after BarTab briefly couldn't add a new section (e.g. in combat). It now retries the section shortly and after combat, and the fallback list never shows alongside the tracker.
- Craft blocks on recipe bars are one color (blue: craftable now, orange: held back by another ingredient) with a thin line (2 screen pixels at any UI scale) between crafts, instead of alternating shades. Short ticks at the top and bottom edges of the rest of the bar mark where each future craft would end, in a darker shade of the color that craft would be once you have the material (blue if the recipe's other ingredients already cover it, orange if not).
- `/bt help` is now a short overview with topics (`/bt help crafting`, `shopping`, `items`, `bars`) that each fit on screen, and covers the newer features (vendors, the auction house tab, recipe chains, item tooltips).
- Recipe chains: "Also track how to make..." now adds one craft's worth; Alt-click it for enough to cover everything the bar is short (the menu shows how many crafts that is).
- Vendors: "Only buy for crafts your bars cover" (Crafting tab, off by default) buys a recipe's vendor materials only for the crafts its tracked bars already cover, e.g. 8 of 10 Heavy Leather for Heavy Armor Kits (5 a kit) buys thread for 1 kit, not 2. Ingredients without a bar (an Iron Buckle you'll get later) don't hold it up.
- Auction house shopping list: a BarTab tab on the auction house's right edge (sharing the tab strip with other addons' tabs) opens a list of what your Crafting and Items bars are short of (recipe ingredients without a bar included), each with a Search button that runs the auction house's own search. Updates as you buy, and reopens if you left it open. On/off on the Crafting tab of the settings. The panel has a solid background, so nameplates behind it don't show through.
- Bars remember their item ID (from links, recipes, your bags or the item cache) and count, match tooltips and match vendor items by it, falling back to the name. Counting is also faster: one bag scan is shared by every bar instead of each bar scanning your bags. Shared bar strings carry the ID.

## 1.5

- Key binding "BarTab: Craft the next ready bar" (Key Bindings > AddOns), also `/bt craft` for macros: crafts the first active recipe bar that can craft now, as a plain click would.
- Settings window reorganized into tabs (General, Crafting, Quests & Items, Display) and made narrower; it opens on the tab you last used.
- BarTab window: width setting (180-400) and a Lock window checkbox in settings (also `/bt window lock|unlock`).
- Item tooltips: hovering an item you track anywhere (bags, bank, auction house, loot, vendors, chat links, profession reagents) adds a line per bar that counts it, e.g. "BarTab: 4/5 for Hillman's Cloak". On/off in settings (Look).
- Vendor materials: at a vendor that sells something your Crafting bars are short of (thread, dye, vials...), including ingredients of tracked recipes that have no bar of their own, BarTab asks whether to buy it, with the amounts and total cost. In settings (Vendors) you can have it buy automatically or turn it off. Only gold purchases, capped at the vendor's stock, your money, and a per-visit spend limit (1g by default; set it in settings). Anything over the limit is left out, and BarTab says what. To leave a recipe (or bar) out, untick "Buy materials at vendors" on its right-click menu.
- Recipe chains: when a bar's material is something you can craft (Heavy Leather from Medium Leather), its tooltip says so and the right-click menu offers "Also track how to make Heavy Leather". That adds bars for the recipe that makes it, sized to what the bar is still short, just above it in the list. BarTab learns your recipes whenever a profession loads.
- Profession headings in BarTab: Crafting show your skill level on the right (e.g. Leatherworking  139/150), updated as you skill up.
- Quest bars under their quests ("inline", /bt inline) are now off by default and marked experimental: they work inside Blizzard's quest-tracker layout, which on Forever could stop quests listed below them from updating. Quest bars show in the BarTab: Quests section instead. Turned off once for everyone; turn it back on in settings if you want it.
- Own window when the objective tracker is hidden (for example by an addon with its own quest tracker): BarTab shows its sections in a window of its own, with the same bars, clicks and menus, and moves back into the tracker when it returns. Drag a section heading to move the window. "Show in" in settings or `/bt window auto|on|off|reset`.
- "After crafting" on a recipe bar's right-click menu: keep the bar (default), move it to Inactive, or untrack it once you've crafted the recipe. Works for crafts from a bar or the profession window, after the whole batch finishes. Shared bar strings carry the choice.
- "Stop tracking" on a recipe bar's right-click menu: untrack the recipe's bars once its profession reaches a skill level (e.g. Hillman's Shoulders at Leatherworking 125), or once the recipe turns yellow, green or gray. Colors are checked whenever the profession is loaded (hovering a bar or opening the window). Shared bar strings carry it. "Ask before removing" in the same menu asks first; No keeps the bars and turns the rule off.

## 1.4

- Alt-Shift-click any item (bags, chat links, loot) to track it. Fill-up items are added right away; anything else opens a small window where you pick Item (Items section), Crafting material, or the open recipe if it uses the item, and set a goal. The bag's "how many" box that Shift opens is closed automatically.
- Session stats in bar tooltips: how many you've gained since login or /reload, the rate per hour, and about how long until the bar fills at that pace. Items taken from the bank don't count. `/bt session reset` starts over.
- Share bar setups as text: `/bt share` (or Share bars in settings, or "Share this bar" / "Share these bars" on the right-click menus) gives a string to paste to a friend; `/bt import` (or Import bars) adds bars from one. Bars you already have take the shared range. Quest and fill-up bars aren't shared.
